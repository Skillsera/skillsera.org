About Skillsera

Skillsera is a community-powered encyclopedia which teaches educational concepts by connecting bite-sized YouTube video explanations.

The Problem

YouTube has an abundance of educational content, but lacks a mechanism for guiding learners through a branching educational journey. Wikipedia excels as a reference but is not designed for deep learning.

The Solution

Skillsera creates a single, unified curriculum — a database of directed dependency edges between YouTube videos, submitted and vetted by the community as a wiki. Each question can have multiple video answers, and each answer can link to prerequisite questions.

Core Principles

  • Video as the fundamental unit of learning
  • Community-driven curation and vetting
  • Open source and non-profit
  • Designed for learners of all backgrounds
  • Long-term digital persistence via archive technologies

Inspired by Metacademy and Khan Academy.
